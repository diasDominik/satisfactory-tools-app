# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 23 October 2026_

Satisfactory Tools ("the app") is a free, unofficial fan project. This policy explains what the app does with data.

## Data the app collects

None. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. It does not send any personal
data to the developer or to anyone else.

## Data stored on your device

Your production lines, folders and settings are saved only on your device. Save files you import and `.sft` files
you open are read on your device and are not uploaded. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes this data.
Android may include it in your own device backup, if you have backups turned on.

## Network access

The app uses the internet for one thing: downloading item and building icons from the Satisfactory wiki
(satisfactory.wiki.gg) and keeping them on the device. These requests contain no personal data, but like any web
request they reveal your IP address to the wiki, whose own privacy policy applies.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

Changes to this policy are published in this file in the app's repository.
