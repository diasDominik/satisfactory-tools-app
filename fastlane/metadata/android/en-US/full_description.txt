A fan-made mobile port of Satisfactory Tools (satisfactorytools.com) by greeny, for planning your factories in Satisfactory 1.0.

Production planner
• Plan several production lines with target rates or "maximize" goals.
• Choose which raw resources, alternate recipes and buildings to use, and add inputs you already have.
• See raw resources, products and byproducts, building counts, build costs and power, plus an interactive production graph.
• The planner runs on your device, so it works offline.
• Import and export .sft files that are compatible with the website.

Codex
• Items, buildings and schematics with recipes, usages and building costs.
• Overclock calculators for manufacturers, extractors and generators.

Also includes a power planner, hard drive research advice and a game version switch between 1.0 and 1.0 + FICSMAS.
